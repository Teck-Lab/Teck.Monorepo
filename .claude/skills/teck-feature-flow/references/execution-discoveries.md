# Discoveries during execution

The approved delivery-manifest digest is frozen scope. Executors report facts;
they never revise the manifest, split Tasks, create issues or dependencies, or
dispatch durable workers. The parent coordinator classifies every discovery.

1. **Approved narrow expansion**: same outcome and reasoning boundary. Continue
   and record the added evidence.
2. **Same outcome, higher complexity**: settle Luna and retry the same Task and
   issue through a fresh Terra/high Dispatch. Do not duplicate durable state.
3. **Bounded defect inside approved scope**: create or reuse the stable repair
   Task and rerun affected review. No architecture pass unless scope,
   dependencies, or acceptance change.
4. **Missing required outcome or changed dependency graph**: dispatch a
   delivery architect on Opus/high or Sol/high. A fresh independent reviewer
   must return CLEAN for the revised manifest digest before any graph mutation.
5. **Required blocker**: reconcile existing state; obtain approved executable
   scope when missing; mirror the directed GitHub/Orca dependency, execute the
   blocker, release its edges, and immediately resume newly eligible Tasks.
6. **Product-scope expansion or unrelated improvement**: record a non-blocking
   follow-up and do not implement it.
7. **Human decision**: open a native Orca decision gate, then revise and review
   the manifest if the answer changes it.

When uncertain, the executor asks and stops editing. It reports failed or
incomplete rather than success. The coordinator does not substitute its own
technical plan for the delivery architect and remains active through every
required architecture, review, blocker, repair, and newly ready Task.
